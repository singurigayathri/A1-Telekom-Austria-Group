# ♛ JobBloom: run your career like a CEO Upload any resume and get: - **Live job ads** (LinkedIn or Indeed via Apify), ranked by AI against your resume - **Target companies** suggested by AI, with job-board links - **What to change** for each job: gaps, missing keywords, rewritten bullets - Everything **under each job** (no separate pitch section): - a **tailored resume**: first a change plan you approve, then the resume. Upload your CV as **.docx** and you get it back **in your own design with your photo** (only the text changes). From a PDF you get a clean ATS version. Strict language check (no EN/DE mixing). - a **cold email**, a **cover letter (Anschreiben)** and a **motivation letter**, each in **English and German** - **HR contacts**: emails and contact persons that the job ads actually publish (never guessed), plus recruiter search links - **Remembers each person**: resume, .docx design and settings are saved in the browser; switch people with the 👤 picker - **Meine Bewerbungen · AMS** (always German): weekly table with Firmenname, Beruf, Gefunden über, Bewerbungsdatum and Status; every "I applied" is logged automatically, and you can add offline applications; export to Excel/CSV or a printable Bewerbungsnachweis - **Your empire**: each application adds a tower to your skyline (XP, titles from Intern → Mogul, achievements) You apply manually. Nothing is sent automatically. **Live site:** https://singurigayathri.github.io/jobBloom/ ## Setup (all free) Open **⚙︎ Settings** on the site. Keys are stored only in your browser, never in this repo, and you can edit or remove them anytime. 1. **AI:** free Gemini key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey (starts with `AQ.` or `AIza`). Anthropic keys also work (paid). 2. **Live jobs (optional):** free Apify account at https://apify.com. Copy the token from Settings → API & Integrations. The free plan includes $5/month of usage. - Default source: `simple.actor/linkedin-jobs` (~$0.30 per 1,000 jobs) - Alternative: `misceres/indeed-scraper` (set the country code, e.g. `AT`) - Or any other Apify actor via **Custom actor**, using an input JSON with `{{query}}`, `{{location}}`, `{{count}}`, `{{country}}`, `{{posted}}` ## Privacy - No server, no database. Everything runs in your browser. - Your resume goes only to the AI provider you choose. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use inputs to improve its products. - Keys are kept for the session only, unless you tick "Remember keys on this device". ## Notes - Scraping job sites can break when they change, so switch the source in Settings if one stops working. Check that the job board's terms are OK for your use. - Single file: `index.html`. Edit it and push, and GitHub Pages redeploys on its own.
